Zero Data Collection • 100% On-Device

Privacy Policy for OrbitForge

Effective Date: November 13, 2026

1. Overview & Data Collection

OrbitForge is an offline 3D aerospace engineering and orbital mechanics simulation game. OrbitForge does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal information, device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics.

2. Local On-Device Storage

All custom rocket blueprints, procedural planetary presets, and simulation settings you create are stored strictly locally on your device using Android's internal app storage (Room SQLite). If Android Auto Backup is enabled on your device, your local database may be backed up to your personal Google Drive account governed by Android's system backup settings.

3. Third-Party SDKs, Ads & Permissions

  • OrbitForge integrates no third-party advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or crash-reporting libraries.
  • OrbitForge requires no account registration or sign-in.
  • OrbitForge requests zero dangerous runtime permissions (no camera, microphone, contacts, or location access).

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data resides solely on your device, you retain complete control over it. You can delete individual saved rocket blueprints or procedural worlds inside the app's Hangar and Worlds menus at any time, or erase all app data immediately by clearing app storage or uninstalling OrbitForge in your Android device settings.

5. Children's Privacy

OrbitForge does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
